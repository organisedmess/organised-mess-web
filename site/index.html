    About Us

    Our process is exploratory but disciplined. We test early, iterate quickly, and develop systems that are materially grounded and technically robust.

    We work across art and commercial contexts — experimental installations, custom hardware systems, spatial interventions. The focus stays the same: clarity of structure, integrity of execution.

    We build a small number of projects each year, moving through research, prototyping, and fabrication — from concept to deployment — and pair the right people with each project, working closely with clients and partners throughout.

    We value transparency, clear communication, and long-term relationships. The best work happens when trust and curiosity are shared.
